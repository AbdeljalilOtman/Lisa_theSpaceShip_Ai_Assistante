## 💡 Tips for Natural Conversation

### DO's ✅
- Speak naturally: "How's the oxygen?" instead of "STATUS OXYGEN"
- Use context: "What about the temperature?" (after asking about pressure)
- Be conversational: "I feel cold" instead of "ADJUST TEMPERATURE"
- Try variations: "Turn on air filter" = "Activate air filtration"

### DON'Ts ❌
- Don't use command syntax: "GET_OXYGEN_LEVEL()"
- Don't shout: "CHECK OXYGEN IMMEDIATELY"
- Don't be too brief: "O2" might not be understood, use "oxygen"
- Don't expect perfection: It's AI, it might need rephrasing

## 🚀 Voice vs. Text Tips

### When to Use Voice 🎤
- ✅ During main demo (shows off the feature)
- ✅ For simple, clear commands
- ✅ When you want to impress judges
- ✅ For environmental and resource queries

### When to Use Text ⌨️
- ✅ In noisy environments
- ✅ For complex or long queries
- ✅ When microphone isn't working
- ✅ For backup during demo
